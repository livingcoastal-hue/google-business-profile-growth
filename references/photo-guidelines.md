# GBP Photo Guidelines

Recommended photo types:

- Team photos.
- Branded vehicle photos.
- Job site photos.
- Before/after photos.
- Equipment photos.
- Storefront or office photos.
- Service-specific photos.
- Community/local photos.
- Trust proof: uniforms, badges, licenses, safety gear.

Upload cadence:

- 3-5 photos per week for active local service businesses.
- Rotate services and locations.
- Prefer authentic field photos over stock images.

Filename pattern:

`business-service-city-short-description.jpg`

Caption pattern:

`[Business] providing [service] in [city/area].`

Do not upload private customer information, license plates, house numbers, faces, or sensitive job details without permission.
