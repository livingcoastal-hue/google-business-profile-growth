# GBP Post Patterns

## Weekly Service Post

Format:

Need help with [service] in [city/area]? [Business] helps local homeowners/businesses with [specific problem]. Call or book online for [CTA].

## Seasonal Tip

Format:

[Season/weather] can cause [problem]. If you notice [symptom], schedule [service] before it becomes [larger issue].

## Project Spotlight

Format:

Recent job in [city/neighborhood]: our team handled [service/problem] and helped the customer [result]. If you need similar help, contact us today.

## Offer

Format:

This week/month, [business] is offering [offer] for [service] in [city/area]. Call [phone] or book online.

## Review Highlight

Format:

Thank you to our customers in [city/area] for trusting us with [service]. We appreciate every review and every chance to serve our community.
