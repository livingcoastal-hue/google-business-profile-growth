# LinkedIn Launch Post

I built a Codex skill + agent bundle for Google Business Profile growth.

It is designed for local businesses and agencies that manage plumbers, HVAC companies, restaurants, med spas, dentists, gyms, salons, law firms, retailers, auto shops, and other local operators.

It can help audit and improve:

- categories
- services
- products and menus
- local competitor gaps
- reviews and responses
- Q&A
- weekly GBP updates
- geo/service keywords
- photo upload plans
- service-area relevance
- profile conversion paths

The agent bundle includes:

- MapScout for local competitor research
- ProfileFixer for GBP optimization
- PostPilot for weekly updates
- PhotoOps for image planning
- ReviewRadar for reputation
- QASeeder for Q&A
- GeoGrid for location strategy
- ActionRunner for browser-assisted edits when the user is logged in

No ranking guarantees. No fake reviews. No spam.

Just a practical operating system for making a Google Business Profile more complete, more useful, and more likely to convert local searchers.
